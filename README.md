# Yield Tokenization and the Emergence of Crypto-Native Fixed Income Markets: Evidence from Pendle

MSc dissertation, FGV-EPGE (Rio de Janeiro), Economics and Corporate Finance, Digital Finance track.

Working draft, October 2026. Final version expected December 2026.

The dissertation examines whether yield tokenization represents a step in the maturation of DeFi toward a crypto-native fixed income market. It reads this new hybrid area through two lenses, finance and technology, taken together, and covers the mechanics of principal and yield tokens (PT, YT, SY), implied rates and term structure, Pendle's AMM design, and the risk and regulatory questions raised by on-chain fixed income.

The PDF in this repository is the current draft.
